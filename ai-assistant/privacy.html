Privacy policy

ai-assistant · effective 5 October 2026

ai-assistant is a personal tool with a single user: its owner, who also operates it on their own computers. This policy describes what it does with data from the owner's Google account.

What it accesses

  • Gmail messages in one label the owner chooses, through the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly.
  • It does not send, delete, label or modify email, and it does not read other Google data.

How it uses the data

  • To summarize email threads in that label and suggest tasks and projects for the owner.
  • Message text is sent to Anthropic's API (Claude) to produce those summaries and suggestions.
  • Summaries and suggestions are posted to the owner's private Discord server for the owner to review.

Where it is stored

  • Summaries, notes and a processing log are stored on the owner's own computers.
  • The Google authorization token is stored in the operating system's keychain on the owner's computer.
  • Data is kept until the owner deletes it.

Sharing

The data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone other than the services named above, which process it only to provide these features to the owner. ai-assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: github.com/tanaes.
