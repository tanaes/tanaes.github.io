ai-assistant

A personal, single-user assistant that its owner runs on their own computers.

ai-assistant keeps its owner's notes, projects and tasks organized. It is a private tool, not a public service: it has one user, it does not offer sign-ups, and nobody else can connect an account to it.

Gmail access

With the owner's permission, ai-assistant reads the messages in one Gmail label that the owner chooses, using Google's read-only Gmail scope. It summarizes those email threads and suggests tasks for the owner to review. It never sends, deletes, labels or changes email.

Details are in the privacy policy.
